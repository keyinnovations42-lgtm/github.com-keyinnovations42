About

Built for builders.

Key Innovations is a construction technology studio building AI-powered tools that actually fit how contractors and builders work.

The person building these tools isn't a startup guy who decided construction looked like an interesting market. He's someone who built houses and commercial structures — who's priced materials, managed crews, and read blueprints on-site. Then he taught himself software development.

That combination is uncommon. The tools reflect it.
